Who I Am

I am a dedicated California-licensed Insurance Producer focused on helping families build lasting financial security through education, protection, and smart planning.

My approach is rooted in the belief that when families understand their options and have the right protections in place, they thrive — and entire communities grow stronger. I serve clients across Delano, Bakersfield, and the greater Central Valley.

I help families with term life insurance and related protection solutions today. I am also preparing to expand into investment and securities products (coming soon) so I can support the full “Buy Term and Invest the Difference” strategy that has helped middle-income families for decades.
