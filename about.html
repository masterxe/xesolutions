The work

I help families get clear on term life protection first: what income replacement actually means, how to size coverage honestly, and how to avoid paying for complexity they don’t need. The conversation is educational before it is transactional.

I work within the scope of my California insurance license. Securities capability is intentionally marked as coming soon until that license is complete — awareness and education can start now; product advice on that side does not.

I also recruit people who want to serve the same way: protect families, stay disciplined, and build a practice that lasts.
