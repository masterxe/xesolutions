COMMUNITY

Stronger students. Clearer families. Same place.

Delano and the Central Valley don’t need more noise. They need people who show up — in the schools and at the kitchen table — with a standard that holds.

Why it matters here

The young people coming up in these valleys will work, lead, and raise families in the same communities — or carry these roots wherever they go. Soft skills are not soft when the environment is demanding. Clarity at home is not optional when one hard season can set a household back for years.

The work is local on purpose. Visibility is not the goal. Stronger people in a stronger place is.

Where the work shows up

In the schools

Youth leadership

After-school POWER program for 8th-grade students at La Viña Middle School — growth mindset, resilience, conflict resolution, empathy, and leadership. Skills meant to transfer into any endeavor they embark in.

With families

Practical clarity

Help local households get clearer on protection and financial decisions — education first, then choices they can stand behind. Product detail lives on Solutions; the commitment is the same.

The standard

Show up. Stay accountable. No empty motivation talk. Whether the room is a middle-school block or a family conversation, the bar does not drop.

Youth work stays separate from the insurance practice. The character of the work does not.

Get involved

Parents, school partners, or neighbors who want to talk about the work on the ground — or families ready for a clearer financial conversation.
