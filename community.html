Community Impact

Financial security is stronger when the whole community rises. Here’s how I show up beyond client work.

Youth Leadership

I lead an after-school POWER program for 8th-grade students focused on building real leadership. We develop growth mindset, resilience, conflict resolution, empathy, and decision-making — skills and habits that transfer into every endeavor they pursue.

Through workshops and one-on-one conversations, I help young families and expanding households understand protection options, budgeting basics, and how to start building security — especially for parents ages 19–30 navigating new responsibilities.
