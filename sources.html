REFERENCES

Sources

Principles discussed on the home page are drawn from the public work of the following educators and authors. This page is a reference list only — the full messaging lives on the home page under Explore.

  1. Williams, A. L. (Art)
    Founder of A.L. Williams. Public advocacy of “buy term and invest the difference” as an alternative to high-cost permanent life insurance for middle-income families. Historical training and industry materials associated with the firm’s founding philosophy.
  2. Orman, Suze
    Personal finance educator and author. Published books and media guidance recommending term life for most households during income-replacement years, with emphasis on understanding costs and ownership of coverage.
  3. Ramsey, Dave
    Personal finance educator and author. Public teaching that prioritizes term life during dependent years, emergency funds, debt reduction, and step-by-step plans aimed at ordinary working families.
  4. Bogle, John C.
    Founder of Vanguard. Books and essays on low-cost investing, index funds, and long-term discipline — relevant to the “invest the difference” side of protection-first planning.
  5. Stanley, Thomas J.
    The Millionaire Next Door and related research on wealth accumulation among ordinary households through discipline, under-consumption, and consistent saving — not high income alone.

Cited for educational and historical context only. No endorsement, affiliation, or sponsorship is claimed. Insurance products offered only where licensed. Securities products not currently offered.
